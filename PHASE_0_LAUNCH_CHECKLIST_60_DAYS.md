---

# 🔵 WEEK 7 — SALES PROCESS AUDIT

Check:

- Qualified lead rate
- Viewing rate
- Close rate
- SLA compliance

If SLA <90% → fix operations, not ads.

---

# 🔵 WEEK 8 — 60-DAY DECISION GATE

Answer:

1. CPL within range?
2. At least 1 sale?
3. At least 3 rentals?
4. Viewing rate >30%?
5. SLA compliance >90%?

If YES → Scale 20–30% budget.

If NO → Diagnose before scaling.

---

# 🚨 STOP CONDITIONS

Pause campaign if:

- CTR <3% after 1,000 impressions
- CPL >2x projected for 7 days
- Conversion tracking fails
- SLA breach >20%
