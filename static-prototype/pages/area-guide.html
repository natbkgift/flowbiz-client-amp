The Wongamat thesis

Wongamat sits on Pattaya's most protected beach stretch. Supply is lower than central Pattaya, tenant retention is higher, and prime towers often cap foreign quota early.

Best for

Long-term capital growth, family residence, European retirees and stable yield.

Watch out for

Foreign quota tightens quickly. Reserve early when a good resale or launch appears.
