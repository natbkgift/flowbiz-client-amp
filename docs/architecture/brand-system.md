\# BRAND SYSTEM — AMP PLATFORM



Version: 1.0

Authority: Brand Governance

Scope: Web / Messaging / UX Copy / Visual Tone / Interaction Style



Purpose:

Lock platform identity, positioning, tone, and perception.

Prevent drift into listing portal, agency marketing, or SaaS UI patterns.



This is not a branding guide.

This is a platform identity contract.



Agent must treat this file as:



```

positioning\_lock

identity\_guardrail

tone\_engine

perception\_boundary

```



Violation → rollback.



---



\# 1) CORE BRAND POSITIONING



AMP is:



> Structured Property Intelligence Platform

> Delivered by Verified Advisors



Not:



\* listing portal

\* brokerage website

\* marketing landing page

\* SaaS dashboard



---



\# 2) IDENTITY ATTRIBUTES



AMP must consistently feel:



\* structured

\* calm

\* intelligent

\* advisory-led

\* credible

\* premium but restrained

\* transparent

\* data-backed



Disallowed identity drift:



\* hype-driven

\* discount-driven

\* urgency-driven

\* promotional tone

\* flashy UI



---



\# 3) PLATFORM ROLE PERCEPTION



Visitors must perceive AMP as:



\* decision infrastructure

\* investment evaluation environment

\* advisory interface



Not:



\* product catalog

\* sales funnel

\* property marketplace



---



\# 4) POSITIONING BOUNDARIES



Disallowed patterns:



```

limited\_time

hot\_project

best\_price

exclusive\_deal

act\_now

only\_today

last\_units

```



These convert AMP into a sales platform.



---



\# 5) LANGUAGE ENGINE



\## Tone



Warm professional.

Measured authority.

Calm clarity.



\## Writing principles



\* structured sentences

\* informative tone

\* non-emotional persuasion

\* data-first messaging

\* human support second



---



\# 6) COPY CONSTRAINTS



Allowed:



\* strategic positioning

\* verified development

\* structured evaluation

\* investment insights

\* advisory support



Disallowed:



\* hype

\* urgency

\* exclamation-driven messaging

\* emotional pressure



---



\# 7) CTA LANGUAGE RULES



Primary CTAs must feel:



\* advisory

\* exploratory

\* controlled



Examples allowed:



\* Start Smart Finder

\* Explore Availability

\* Review Investment Details

\* Book Private Viewing

\* Speak with Advisor



Disallowed:



\* Buy Now

\* Reserve Now

\* Don’t Miss

\* Limited Time

\* Last Chance



---



\# 8) VISUAL IDENTITY CONTRACT



Visual system must communicate:



\* intelligence

\* calm authority

\* editorial curation



Disallowed visual patterns:



\* flashy gradients

\* aggressive contrast

\* animation-heavy UI

\* sales badge overlays



---



\# 9) COLOR AUTHORITY



Primary colors must signal:



\* authority

\* clarity

\* calm



Accent colors must:



\* remain minimal

\* never dominate UI

\* never drive urgency



Gold reserved for:



\* structural separation

\* editorial cues

\* premium rhythm



---



\# 10) TYPOGRAPHY ROLE



Display:



\* authority

\* stability



Body:



\* clarity

\* readability



Numeric:



\* precision

\* investment credibility



Typography must not:



\* feel playful

\* feel marketing-heavy

\* feel consumer retail



---

\* consultant

\* evaluator

\* investment guide



Not:



\* salesperson

\* closer

\* promoter



---



\# 12) PROJECT POSITIONING



Project must appear as:



\* investment case

\* evaluation subject

\* structured opportunity



Not:



\* product for sale

\* discount item

\* promotional campaign

\# 13) HERO BRAND CONTRACT



Hero must communicate:



\* intelligence

\* calm authority

\* advisory support

\* data credibility



Hero must NOT:



\* shout

\* sell

\* push urgency

\* feel campaign-driven

Consistency required between:

\* Listing
