# AMP Verified Project Ad Winners (Researched Examples)

Last updated: 2026-03-06  
Method: Curated from primary sources (Google/Think with Google + official help docs)

## 1) Verified Case Examples

## Example A - Zoopla (UK property platform, 2025)

What they ran:
- Demand Gen across YouTube, Shorts, Discover, Gmail
- AI optimization + life-event audiences + lookalikes
- Short video cut-downs from brand campaign with direct action CTA

Reported outcomes:
- +14% valuation searches (seller intent signal)
- +4.2% ad recall lift
- +1.1% brand awareness lift
- +28% leads

How this maps to AMP:
- Use short project videos (10s cut-downs) with clear CTA to message.
- Run demand-gen style creative for users still in early intent stage.

Source:
- https://business.google.com/en-all/think/ai-excellence/zoopla-youtube-ai-measurement-case-study/

## Example B - Haya Real Estate (Spain, 2019)

What they ran:
- Dynamic Search Ads (DSA) to cover long-tail property searches by municipalities
- Category-based campaign structure

Reported outcomes:
- +5% additional leads
- CPL up to 52% lower than account average
- +9% Search impressions

How this maps to AMP:
- For phase 2 (website + Google), use DSA/page feed on project and area pages.
- Good fit for high URL volume (project pages, area pages, intent pages).

Source:
- https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/intl/es-es/estrategias-de-marketing/busqueda/haya-real-estate-consigue-leads-un-52-mas-economicos-con-campana-de-dynamic-search-ads/

## Example C - Toll Brothers (Luxury home builder, US, 2014)

What they ran:
- In-market audiences + remarketing together on Google Display
- Similar Audiences and contextual expansion to feed remarketing pool

Reported outcomes:
- Remarketing CPA lowered by up to 50%
- Remarketing CTR up to 3x
- More remarketing leads and larger list volume

How this maps to AMP:
- Keep prospecting + retargeting separated.
- Build qualified remarketing pools from intent pages and chat starts.

Source:
- https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/_qs/documents/472/toll-brothers-builds-qualified-leads-with-the-google-display-network_case-studies.pdf

## Example D - Apartments.com (US rentals platform, 2016)

What they ran:
- Mobile-focused expanded text ads + price extensions (now equivalent to modern asset-driven search formats)
- City-level rollout across 1,000 markets

Reported outcomes:
- CTR +10-15% from expanded text ads
- CTR +120% when price extensions were present
- Website traffic +24%

How this maps to AMP:
- For project/resale/rental search ads, expose price bands and specifics in ad assets.
- Message-match headline -> listing detail -> chat CTA.

Source:
- https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/_qs/documents/1655/apartments-dot-com-makes-move-price-extensions-expanded-text-ads.pdf

## Example E - Private Property (South Africa property portal, 2015)

What they ran:
- Rich-media display formats (engagement-led creative) instead of standard banners
- Integrated online + offline campaign

Reported outcomes:
- Click volume +319%
- Engagement rate +386%
- CPC (or engagement cost) -64%
- Users were 10x more likely to engage than with standard banners

How this maps to AMP:
- For project launches, use richer visual formats and story-driven creative.
- Standard static banners alone will likely underperform for discovery audiences.

Source:
- https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/_qs/documents/2677/Case_Study-_Private_Property_2015_ZA-EN_1.pdf

## 2) Practical Playbook for AMP Project Ads

## Pattern 1 - New Project Demand Capture (Top 3 Priority Projects)

Use for:
- ZENITH II Pattaya
- The Riviera Plam Beach Wongamat
- EMBASSY LIFE

Setup:
- Goal: message starts + qualified chats
- Creative: 10-20s video + 1 carousel variant
- Audience split: broad, lookalike, warm retarget

Primary message:
- "Send budget + timeline, get a focused shortlist in chat"

## Pattern 2 - Discovery Funnel (Not Sure Yet)

Use for:
- Users undecided between invest/live/holiday

Setup:
- Creative shows intent map, not just property images
- CTA keyword: START
- Route all leads to discovery desk for qualification

Primary message:
- "Start from your goal, then we match project type"

## Pattern 3 - Search Intent Capture (Phase 2+)

Use for:
- High-intent queries by area/project type

Setup:
- Search + DSA with URL/page feed
- Include price-related assets where possible
- Landing page CTA remains message-first

Primary message:
- "Compare project options by budget and timeline"

## 3) Measurement Rules (Non-negotiable)

- Track by `locale`, `lead_type`, `offer_family`, `campaign`, `intent`.
- Separate reporting for prospecting vs retargeting.
- Do not optimize purely on lead volume; optimize on qualified chat rate and CPQC.
- Keep `call_requested:no` default unless user explicitly requests a call.
