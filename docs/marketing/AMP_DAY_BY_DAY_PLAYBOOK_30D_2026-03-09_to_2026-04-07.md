## Weekly Decision Ritual (Every Monday)

- Keep: ad sets with strong qualified rate + stable CPQC
- Fix: ad sets with good volume but poor quality
- Kill: ad sets with weak quality for 7 consecutive days
- Scale: top performers by controlled 20-30% budget steps

- Top 3 winning hooks + top 3 objections log
- List of leads requiring next-day priority handling
- Confirmation that call-by-request policy had no violations
