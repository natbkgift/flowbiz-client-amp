## SECTION 1 — MARKET POSITIONING

### 1.1 Core Positioning Statement

> **AMP Pattaya is the acquisition-focused real estate agency that removes the legal ambiguity, language barriers, and transactional risk from buying, investing in, or renting condominiums in Pattaya — specifically for English-speaking foreign nationals.**

**Reasoning:** The Pattaya condo market is saturated with agencies that speak *to* foreigners but do not speak *for* them. Most agencies lead with property listings. AMP must lead with **transactional clarity** — the single highest anxiety point for foreign buyers. This positions AMP not as a listing aggregator but as a **transaction partner**.

### 1.2 Primary Target Segments

| Segment | Profile | Intent | Avg. Budget (THB) | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Retirement Buyer** | 55–72, UK/EU/AUS/US, relocating or snowbirding | Buy to live | 2M–6M | 🔴 HIGH |
| **Yield Investor** | 35–55, seeking 5–8% gross rental yield, remote decision-maker | Buy to rent | 3M–10M | 🔴 HIGH |
| **Digital Nomad / Long-stay Renter** | 28–45, remote workers, 3–12 month stays | Rent | 15K–45K/mo | 🟡 MEDIUM |
| **Relocating Expat** | 30–50, job-based relocation or trailing spouse | Buy or Rent | 2M–8M (buy) / 20K–60K/mo (rent) | 🟡 MEDIUM |

**Reasoning:** Retirement buyers and yield investors represent the highest transaction value and clearest intent signals in search behavior. Digital nomads have lower transaction value but generate consistent rental pipeline volume. Relocating expats are opportunistic — captured via same content, not targeted separately in early campaigns.

### 1.3 Key Pain Points for Foreign Buyers

| # | Pain Point | Severity | Implication for AMP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Foreign ownership quota confusion** — "Can I even own this?" | CRITICAL | Must address above the fold on every page |
| 2 | **Transfer process opacity** — sinking fund, common fees, tax at transfer | HIGH | Dedicated content block required |
| 3 | **Trust deficit** — "Is this agent legitimate or a tout?" | HIGH | Social proof + licensing + face-on-site required |
| 4 | **Price manipulation fear** — "Am I paying a foreigner premium?" | HIGH | Transparent pricing strategy required |
| 5 | **Legal representation gap** — "Do I need a lawyer? Which one?" | MEDIUM | Legal partner co-branding opportunity |
| 6 | **Remote decision-making friction** — "I can't fly over just to look" | MEDIUM | Video walkthrough + virtual viewing offer |
| 7 | **Rental yield skepticism** — "Are those numbers real?" | MEDIUM | Must show verifiable yield data, not projections |

### 1.4 Unique Value Proposition

**For Buyers:**
> "We handle the legal structure, foreign quota verification, and full transfer process — in English, with full cost transparency — so you buy with certainty, not hope."

**For Renters:**
> "Verified units, direct pricing, and a move-in process designed for people who don't speak Thai and don't have 6 weeks to figure it out."

**Reasoning:** The UVP is not about "beautiful condos" — every agency says that. The UVP is about **removing transactional friction**. This is the gap in the Pattaya market. Agencies like Fazwaz, Hipflat, and PropertyScout compete on listing volume. AMP competes on **execution confidence**.

### 1.5 Competitive Differentiation Strategy

| Competitor Type | Their Approach | AMP Differentiation |
|---|---|---|
| Portal aggregators (Fazwaz, DDProperty) | Volume listings, weak service layer | AMP = curated + full-service transaction support |
| Local Thai agencies | Thai-first, English as afterthought | AMP = English-first, foreigner-first process |
| Expat-run agencies | Personality-driven, inconsistent | AMP = process-driven, repeatable |
| Developer sales offices | Push own stock only | AMP = market-wide access, unbiased |

**Core Differentiator:** AMP is the only agency in Pattaya that structures its entire client experience around the **foreign buyer's transaction anxiety** — not around property beauty.

| Do NOT | Reason |
|---|---|
| Run Display Network campaigns | Low intent, burns budget, pollutes conversion data |

| Use broad match keywords | Will match irrelevant queries; use phrase match + exact match only |
| Create more than 4 campaigns | Overcomplicates management, splits data too thin |

| Bid on Thai competitor brand names | Legal risk + low conversion, waste of Phase 0 budget |
| Launch ads before landing pages pass manual QA | One broken form = weeks of lost data |

### 4.6 Budget Allocation Logic

**Assumption:** Phase 0 test budget = 60,000–120,000 THB/month (≈ $1,700–$3,400 USD/month)

| Campaign | Budget % | Monthly THB (at 90K avg) | Logic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buy Condo | 40% | 36,000 | Highest transaction value |
| Investment | 30% | 27,000 | High value, smaller volume |
| Rent Condo | 20% | 18,000 | Volume play, lower per-lead value |

**Week 1–2:** Run all campaigns at equal daily budget to collect baseline data.

**Bid Strategy:** Start with Manual CPC for 2 weeks to understand auction dynamics. Move to Target CPA only after 30+ conversions are recorded.

## SECTION 5 — FUNNEL KPI MODEL

### 5.1 Metric Targets

| Stage | Metric | Target | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ad → Click** | CTR | 4–7% | High-intent exact/phrase match keywords in a niche vertical should yield above-average CTR. Below 4% = ad copy or keyword mismatch. |
| **Click → Lead** | Landing Page Conversion Rate | 5–10% | Industry real estate average is 2–4%. Targeted landing pages with strong trust elements and specific foreign-buyer messaging should outperform. Target 5% conservatively, optimize toward 10%. |
| **Lead → Qualified** | Lead Qualification Rate | 40–60% | Not all leads will be genuine buyers. Some will be curious, some will be agents fishing. A well-structured intake form (budget, timeline, purpose) filters at point of capture. |
| **Qualified → Viewing** | Viewing Rate | 30–50% | Dependent on speed of response and WhatsApp follow-up discipline. Foreign buyers who are in Pattaya convert to viewings at high rates if contacted within 30 minutes. Remote buyers convert to video viewings at ~20–30%. |
| **Viewing → Close** | Closing Rate | 5–15% (Buy) / 15–25% (Rent) | Buy closings are elongated (30–90 days). Rental closings are faster (1–14 days). These rates apply over the full decision cycle, not same-day. |

## SECTION 6 — SALES PROCESS DISCIPLINE

### 6.1 Lead Response Time Requirement

| Lead Source | Maximum Response Time | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp message | **5 minutes** during business hours (9am–8pm ICT) | WhatsApp |
| Form submission | **15 minutes** during business hours | WhatsApp (first), then email |
| After hours | **First thing next morning (9am)** | WhatsApp |

**Rule:** Every lead that waits more than 30 minutes loses 50%+ of its conversion potential. This is the single most important operational metric in Phase 0.

**Implementation:** Assign one person as "lead duty" for each shift. This is not optional. No lead should sit unacknowledged.

### 6.2 Follow-Up Timeline

```
MINUTE 0–5:    First contact (WhatsApp)
                → Acknowledge inquiry
                → Confirm you received their request
                → Ask 1 qualifying question

HOUR 1–2:      Send relevant information
                → Property shortlist (Buy/Invest)
                → Available units list (Rent)
                → Personalized, not a blast

DAY 1:         Follow up if no response
                → "Did you get a chance to review the options I sent?"
                → Offer video call or video walkthrough

DAY 3:         Second follow-up
                → Share one new/relevant listing
                → Add value, not just "checking in"

DAY 7:         Third follow-up
                → Share market insight or lead magnet
                → "Thought this might be useful for your search"

DAY 14:        Fourth follow-up
                → Direct question: "Are you still looking?"
                → If no response → move to monthly check-in

DAY 30+:       Monthly check-in
                → 1 message per month with new inventory or market update
                → Stop after 3 months of no response
```

**Rule:** Never send "Just checking in" without attaching value. Every message must contain either a listing, a data point, or a useful resource.

### 6.4 Viewing Scheduling Structure

```
STEP 1: Confirm interest in specific units (minimum 2, maximum 5 per viewing session)
STEP 2: Propose 2 time slots within 48 hours
STEP 3: Send confirmation message with:
         → Date, time
         → Meeting point (lobby of first property OR AMP office)
         → Agent name + photo
         → Unit list with addresses
         → Estimated duration (1.5–2 hours for 3 units)
STEP 4: Send reminder 2 hours before viewing
STEP 5: After viewing → same-day follow-up message:
         → "Thank you for viewing today"
         → Summary of units seen
         → "Which one resonated most?"
         → Next step (cost breakdown / second viewing / reservation)
```

**For Remote Buyers (Video Viewing):**
```
→ Schedule via WhatsApp video call or Google Meet
→ Agent physically at the unit with stable internet
→ Walk through unit, building facilities, surrounding area
→ Record and send video after call (buyer keeps for reference)
→ Follow up with cost breakdown same day
```

### 6.5 Objection Handling Framework

| Objection | Response Strategy |
|---|---|
| "I'm just looking / not ready yet" | "Completely understand. Let me send you our Foreign Buyer's Guide — it covers the full process so you're prepared when you're ready. I'll also flag any new listings that match your criteria." |
| "I need to visit first" | "That makes total sense. When are you planning to be in Pattaya? I can have a curated shortlist ready for viewings when you arrive. In the meantime, I can send video walkthroughs of the top options." |
| "The price seems high" | "Let me show you the price comparison data for [area] — here's what similar units have sold/rented for in the last 6 months. I can also highlight units that offer better value for your criteria." |
| "I'm worried about the legal process" | "That's the most common concern for foreign buyers. Here's exactly how the process works: [explain quota, transfer, legal review]. We also coordinate with [legal partner name] who specializes in foreign purchases." |
| "I've heard bad things about buying in Thailand" | "There are risks — and we'll be transparent about every one of them. The key is proper due diligence: quota verification, contract review, and working with a licensed agency. That's exactly what we provide." |
| "Your commission is too high" / "Can I deal direct?" | "Our commission is standard at [X%] and is paid by the seller/developer. There's no additional cost to you as a buyer. We earn our fee by managing the full process — quota check, legal coordination, transfer support." |
| "I found cheaper on [other site]" | "Happy to check that listing for you. We often find that listed prices don't include transfer fees, common area fees, or the unit may not have foreign quota available. Let me verify and give you the real total cost comparison." |

## END OF PHASE 0 — DECISION GATE

At Day 60, answer these questions before proceeding to any next phase:

1. **Is cost per qualified lead within acceptable range?** (Target: <1,500 THB for buy, <500 THB for rent)
2. **Are leads being responded to within SLA?** (Target: 100% within 15 minutes during business hours)
3. **Are viewings converting from qualified leads?** (Target: >30%)
4. **Have we closed at least 1 sale and 3 rentals?** (Minimum viability proof)
5. **Is every published listing verified and current?** (Target: 100%)

If YES to all 5 → Proceed to Phase 1 (scale traffic, add channels).
If NO to any → Fix the broken element. Do not scale.
